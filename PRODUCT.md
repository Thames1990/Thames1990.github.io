# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers and recruiters evaluating Thomas Mohr for a technical project leadership or engineering leadership role.

## Product Purpose

Present Thomas Mohr's experience, selected work, and curriculum vitae so hiring teams can assess his fit and contact him about a role.

## Positioning

Thomas bridges project direction and hands-on engineering, contributing from planning through production.

## Operating Context

Hiring teams may scan the portfolio on desktop or mobile, inspect concrete project evidence, review the full CV, and follow up by email or LinkedIn.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The statically generated Astro site is deployed to GitHub Pages.
- `/` presents the portfolio and selected projects; `/cv` presents the full CV and provides a PDF download at `/cv.pdf`.
- Copy may be rewritten, but career and project facts must remain accurate.
- Preserve working contact routes, CV/PDF download, accessible semantics, and responsive behavior.

## Brand Commitments

The site has no established visual identity. The user authorizes replacing its layout and visual design without treating its current blue styling as a commitment.

## Evidence on Hand

- The full CV and career history in `src/data/cv.ts`.
- Three portfolio case studies: Audiomatik Loader, Lufthansa Fuel & Leg Twin, and the 80+ TB music catalog platform.
- Existing case-study outcomes and project links in `src/data/cv.ts`.
- Do not invent career facts, customer endorsements, or project outcomes.

## Product Principles

- Make the bridge between leadership and hands-on engineering tangible.
- Use accurate, specific project evidence to earn confidence.
- Let hiring teams quickly understand fit, then offer deeper detail.
- Keep contacting Thomas and accessing the full CV straightforward.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
